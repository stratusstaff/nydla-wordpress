# NYDLA.com Home page — plan

**Live staging:** https://merciful-darkturquoise-giraffe-37.hostingersite.com/  
**Theme code:** https://github.com/stratusstaff/nydla-wordpress  

This is a plan only. Nothing on this list is “build it now” until we say go.

---

## What the old site (NYDLA.org) is

- Theme: **GeneratePress**
- Page builder: **Divi**
- Homepage is basically:
  1. A big top **carousel** (podcasts + sponsor promos)
  2. **Our Mission** (Thomas quote + cloud / association text)
  3. Welcome / mission paragraphs (579M+ people, NYDLA | NADLA)
  4. **Sponsor logos** + push to sponsorship

We’re keeping those *ideas*. We’re **not** copying Divi or the old layout look.

---

## What we’re building on the new theme

**Home page content only.**  
Do **not** change the site header or footer in this pass (that’s a separate to-do later).

Make it **modern and cleaner** than nydla.org — same jobs, nicer look.

### Section order (top to bottom)

**1. Big “Page 1 of NYDLA” carousel**  
Full-width first screen. A few strong slides (skyline / brand). Not a pile of random promo banners.

**2. Our Mission**  
Use Thomas’ framing and the real mission copy, including:

- “Our services are a compilation of the most reliable cloud products and services available.”
- Welcome to NYDLA & NADLA, serving 579M+ people
- The full NYDLA | NADLA mission paragraph
- Knowledge + technology + talent (the three pillars idea)

**3. Sponsor logos carousel**  
Thank sponsors / vendors. Logo strip can be a **placeholder** for now — we’ll make our own sponsors plugin later.

**4. Sponsorship CTA**  
“Learn more about our Sponsorship” → button to **Sponsorship Plans**  
(For now that page can be a simple **Coming soon** page.)
